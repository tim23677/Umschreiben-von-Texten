# Persona write

Use when the user requests writing or editing in a specified voice, or asks for help with generic, padded prose. The goal is a defined, appropriate voice—not a generic imitation of human writing.

## Resolve the voice

Use a persona the user names or describes. If none is provided and the choice materially affects the result, ask one concise question. Do not invent a real person's private style or claim exact impersonation; use broad traits such as concise, warm, technical, or skeptical.

## Short-text workflow

1. Extract purpose, audience, format, point, and constraints.
2. List the facts, claims, names, numbers, and uncertainty that must remain.
3. Identify the chosen voice's broad traits and apply them without exaggeration.
4. Draft or revise locally; preserve useful original wording and genre structure.
5. Check factual fidelity, tone, and readability before returning the complete result.

## Long documents

Work section by section. Keep a compact record of terminology, facts, voice choices, and unresolved questions, then run a consistency pass across sections. Do not force a full-document rewrite in one pass when it risks losing detail.

Never invent experience or evidence, add fake human quirks, or optimize for AI detector evasion. For audit-only requests, diagnose without rewriting.
