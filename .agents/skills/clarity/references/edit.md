# Editing guide

Use this for rewrites and as the pattern reference during reviews. Read passages in context; familiar constructions are not defects when they are earned or required by the medium.

## Order of work

1. **Truth and scope:** protect claims, attribution, uncertainty, examples, citations, links, conditions, quotations, and required structure.
2. **Substance:** identify the point and the source material the author supplied. Ask or mark a limitation when it is absent.
3. **Development:** connect paragraphs through cause, contrast, sequence, qualification, example, or consequence.
4. **Sentences:** remove inflated, repetitive, or formulaic wording while preserving voice and medium conventions.
5. **Craft:** restore concrete material, stance, warmth, or rhythm if editing made the piece colder or flatter.

## Useful diagnoses

- **Importance without mechanism:** replace unsupported emphasis with a supported actor, mechanism, result, or limit.
- **Vague specificity:** do not fill missing details with invented anecdotes or facts; ask or cut.
- **Vague attribution:** name the supplied source, retain uncertainty, ask, or remove the claim.
- **Formula carrying the argument:** test whether the claim remains without the rhetorical cadence and whether its implied relation is supported.
- **Abstract actors:** name the person, team, system, or process when it improves clarity; retain passive voice when appropriate.
- **Structural regularity:** fix the dominant problem; do not add randomness just to appear irregular.
- **Surface residue:** stock openers, empty emphasis, decorative tails, generic endings, and unnecessary formatting are search aids, not a ban list.

Preserve useful details, genuine asides, trade language, deliberate repetition, and earned rhythm. Do not add these as “human signals.”

## Rewrite check

Confirm every factual claim, number, quote, citation, attribution, condition, and link remains accurate; no new experience or causal claim appeared; and the medium still works. If it fails, repair once, then stop.
