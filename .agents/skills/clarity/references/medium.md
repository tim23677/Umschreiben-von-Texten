# Medium routing

Read only the row relevant to the task. Explicit requirements and binding house or venue rules take priority.

| Medium | Optimize for | Preserve | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essay, article, newsletter | Development, judgment, examples, momentum | Voice, uncertainty, earned digressions | Generic surveys without a through-line |
| Documentation, guide | Correct task completion, scanning, failure states | Headings, lists, code, warnings, exact terms | Hiding conditions or restructuring for novelty |
| Reference, API, policy | Retrieval, precision, consistency | Schemas, definitions, normative language | Anti-template edits that impede comparison |
| Academic | Claim-evidence fit and venue conventions | Citations, numbers, calibrated uncertainty | Invented evidence or casualizing |
| Legal, medical, safety | Accuracy, scope, traceable authority | Notices, definitions, escalation paths | Dropping protections for brevity |
| Marketing | Specific audience, demonstrated value | Product names, constraints, calls to action | Unsupported claims and generic excitement |
| Email, memo, chat | Request, decision, owner, next action | Social context and useful bullets | Turning a short message into an essay |
| UI | Task completion, brevity, accessibility | Labels, recovery, localization | Personality that changes semantics |
| Fiction, narrative | Perspective, causality, scene, genre | Deliberate ambiguity and voice | Manufactured disorder |

Never invent or alter evidence. Consult the governing source for current legal, medical, policy, or venue requirements.
