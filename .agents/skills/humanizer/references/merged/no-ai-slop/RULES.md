# No AI slop

Use this for an editorial audit or revision, not to decide who wrote a text. Do not score a draft or guess whether it came from a person, Claude, or another model.

## Audit mode

When asked to audit without rewriting, identify concrete passages, name the observable issue, explain why it may weaken the piece, and suggest a focused fix. Do not infer authorship.

## Edit mode

1. Read the whole draft and identify its purpose, audience, and voice.
2. Preserve claims, facts, names, figures, quotations, links, citations, uncertainty, and scope.
3. Remove only unnecessary filler, repetition, vague claims, or awkward syntax. Keep useful repetition and genre conventions.
4. Do not invent examples, evidence, anecdotes, opinions, or personal experience. Ask when essential information is missing.
5. Return the complete edit and a brief note about material changes.

Never add deliberate errors, forced slang, fake personal details, or claim that a revision will pass an AI detector. A pattern list can guide editing but cannot identify authorship.
